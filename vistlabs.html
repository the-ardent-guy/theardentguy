VIST Labs

Repositioning a post-harvest technology company for banks and growers

VIST Labs, based in Illinois, makes patented post-harvest technology for cannabis growers. I redesigned its website so banks would see a regulated technology company, and growers would still see the fix for failed microbial tests.

Role
Product Designer (solo), at Bleed & Margins
Timeline
3 months, remote, with stakeholders in Illinois, USA
Platform
Website, built on Shopify
My work
Stakeholder research, positioning, information architecture, wireframes, visual design, Shopify build and handover

The brief was one line: look more pharmaceutical. The hard part was speaking to a conservative lender and a grower on one site without losing either.

The problem

Cannabis is legal in Illinois but still federally illegal, and 89% of community banks have no plans to serve the industry (RMA, 2024). VIST's banks needed to see a technology company.

The old site opened with "Cannabis Remediation", sold through fear imagery like mould close-ups and "Would you inhale that?", and buried five patented processes in one long scroll.

How might we present VIST as a regulated technology company to banks, while keeping it the obvious choice for growers?

Research

I ran sessions with the founders, researched federal and FinCEN banking rules, audited the old site's content, and reviewed competitors. Rad Source, Willow Industries and Ziel all market openly to cannabis, which VIST's banks couldn't accept. That left three audiences:

AudienceNeeds to seeLands on
Banks A technology company that meets international standards Home, About
Growers Proof it works and passes state testing Solutions
Finance teams How to buy: own, lease, rent or subscribe Opportunities

Key decisions

01

Two-tier messaging

The homepage speaks to the most conservative reader, with phrases like "plant-based industries" and "pharmaceutical-grade standards". Cannabis appears one level down, on a Markets Served page.

A vocabulary map gave each service a bank-safe name and a grower-facing meaning: CryoPasteurization is "natural microbial load reduction" on the homepage and "decontamination for cannabis" for growers.

Trade-off: The homepage gives up search traffic for "cannabis decontamination", which the blog and solution pages pick up instead.

02

Structure built on problem, solution, then buying

The homepage moves from the problem, to what VIST does, to how to buy. Each of the five services got its own product page from one template: proof points, how it works, before and after, and a consultation call to action.

The testing kits page was built to switch from enquiry to add-to-cart without a redesign, and buying options moved to their own Opportunities section.

Trade-off: One template gives less room to tailor each page to its technology.

03

Evidence in place of fear

I replaced the fear imagery with claims a lender can check: 99.9% microbial reduction, no chemicals or radiation, and terpene preservation. A social proof block added certifications, awards, client logos and team credentials, and the photography moved to the equipment and the process.

Trade-off: The emotional pull on growers was lost, so a before and after section on each product page keeps a controlled version of it.

04

Shopify over WordPress

Shopify won on three counts: a native checkout ready for the testing kits, solid SEO defaults, and content the client could edit without a developer.

Outcome

The site launched on vistlabs.com as the client's primary site, and the founders used it with their customers. VIST later changed direction and went back to its old site.

I delivered the positioning framework, a sitemap of 6 sections and 12 sub-pages, wireframes, a design language, the live Shopify build and the handover.

Reflection

  • Set up analytics before launch. Without a baseline, I can't show whether the repositioning improved lead quality.
  • Test the language with real readers. I validated the two-tier messaging only with the founders. Ten minutes each with a banker and a grower would have tested it directly.
