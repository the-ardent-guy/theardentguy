Drop the music-disc track here as:

    november-rain.mp3

The player (bottom-left rotating disc on the homepage) points at
/assets/audio/november-rain.mp3. Until the file exists the disc still
spins and the title bar still swipes out on hover; clicking play just
finds nothing to load. Any .mp3 works — rename it november-rain.mp3,
or change the <audio src> in index.html to match your filename.
